What to expect from a result

We match transactions against a merchant catalog and use AI for some lookups. A result can be incomplete, wrong, or unresolved. A merchant match does not prove that a payment is legitimate, and a location result does not prove where a person was.

Check results before using them for decisions that affect someone’s money or access to services. Ultrafinance does not provide financial, tax, or legal advice, and is not a substitute for your bank’s transaction records.
